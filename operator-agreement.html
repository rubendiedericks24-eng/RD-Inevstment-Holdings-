Operator Agreement (POPIA)

NexAgent AI — RD Investment Holdings (Pty) Ltd  |  Last updated: 16 October 2026

In short: your customers' information belongs to your business. We only use it to run NexAgent AI for you, we keep it safe, we tell you straight away if something goes wrong, and we delete it when you leave.

1. Who this agreement is between

Us (the operator): RD Investment Holdings (Pty) Ltd, trading as NexAgent AI, a private company registered in South Africa with CIPC, registration number 2026/237915/07. Director and Information Officer (registered with the Information Regulator): Ruben Diedericks. Address: 44 Greenock Road, Gqeberha, Eastern Cape, 6025, South Africa. Email: admin@rdinvestmentholdings.co.za. WhatsApp: +27 72 840 0352.

You (the responsible party): the business that signs up for NexAgent AI ("the Client").

Roles: For your customers' information, you are the responsible party and we are your operator. You decide why and how your customers' information is used. We process it for you.

For our own information about you (your contact people, our leads, our invoices and billing ledger including top-ups), we are the responsible party. Our Privacy Policy covers that, not this agreement.

This agreement forms part of our Terms of Service. If they conflict on how your customers' information is handled, this agreement wins.

3. We follow your instructions

We only process your customers' information to deliver NexAgent AI as set up for you, and on your other reasonable written instructions (email or WhatsApp is fine). If an instruction looks unlawful to us, we will tell you and may refuse it. If the law forces us to disclose information, we will tell you first unless the law stops us.

4. Confidentiality

We treat your customers' information as confidential. Only our people who need it to run or support your service can see it (currently our director and our operations and technical staff). Everyone with access is bound to keep it confidential, also after they leave us.

6. Security

Access is restricted to authorised personnel. Data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.

Each client's records are kept separately for each client, owned and controlled by NexAgent AI; clients access them via the dashboard and WhatsApp, and receive exports on request. Access is restricted and protected with strong authentication where available. Data stored with Google is encrypted at rest by Google.

7. Information sent outside South Africa

All five vendors are outside South Africa, so your customers' information is processed abroad. We only use vendors that are bound by data protection terms (their published data processing terms or standard contractual clauses) that give protection similar to POPIA.

8. If something goes wrong (data breach)

If we have reasonable grounds to believe someone unauthorised has accessed or taken your customers' information, we will tell you immediately (WhatsApp/phone and email). We will tell you what happened, what information is involved, what we have done to contain it, and who to contact.

Under POPIA it is your duty, as responsible party, to notify the Information Regulator and affected customers as soon as reasonably possible. We will help you: we give you the facts, a draft notice, and help with the Information Regulator's eServices portal.

9. Helping with customer requests

If one of your customers asks to see, correct or delete their information, or objects to its use, we will help you deal with it. If the request comes to us directly, we will send it to you within 5 business days and will not answer it ourselves unless you ask us to. We will make the change you instruct within a reasonable time. During the service, you may ask us to correct or delete records at any time, on request by WhatsApp or email, or via the dashboard, and we do it within a reasonable time. When the service ends, your client file and its version history are deleted.

10. When the service ends

Export during the service: you may ask for a full CSV/Excel export of your data at any time during the service. We deliver it within 5 business days.

When your service ends, you may ask in writing for an export of your data within 30 days. After that we delete your customers' information from our systems and ask our vendors to delete it, within 30 days of the end of service, unless the law requires us to keep it.

Exception: our own billing records about you (invoices and the billing ledger, including top-ups) are our records, not your customers' data. We keep them for as long as tax law requires (see our Privacy Policy).

11. Your duties

As responsible party you agree to:

  • Have a lawful reason to collect and use your customers' information (for example, they asked you for a quote).
  • Tell your customers who you are and that NexAgent helps run your chat (we give you a short WhatsApp greeting for this).
  • Only send marketing messages to customers who agreed, or existing customers who can opt out, and honour every STOP or opt-out.
  • Only give us instructions that are lawful.
  • Tell us quickly if you think something is wrong.

12. Liability

Each party is responsible for its own breaches of POPIA and this agreement. Our total liability under this agreement is limited in the same way as the Liability section of our Terms of Service. Nothing here limits liability that the law does not allow to be limited.

13. Acceptance

You accept this agreement when you sign up for NexAgent AI, sign our service agreement, or start using the service. It lasts as long as we process your customers' information. Sections 4, 8, 9 and 10 continue after the service ends until all your customers' information is deleted.

We may update this agreement with 14 days' notice by email.
