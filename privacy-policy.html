10. Fees, payment and refunds

One plan. Founder (first 10): R2,999 setup + R1,999 per month, locked for 12 months, then R2,499 per month with 30 days' notice. First payment R4,998. Standard: R4,999 setup + R2,499 per month. ZAR, exclusive of VAT unless stated.

Each month includes 300 AI chats and 60 jobs (a job is one quote-to-paid cycle). Usage is tracked per client and shown on the dashboard, with alerts at 80% and 100%. At 100% the AI keeps replying (never cut off) and we ask you to top up: one R350 top-up pack adds +250 chats and +50 jobs, one charge on the next invoice. Usage resets monthly. The Client pays its own WhatsApp (Meta) messaging fees on its own Meta billing card. See Fair Use and Usage in the Terms of Service.

Payment is EFT into the NexAgent AI bank account. No debit order. The retainer for a month must reflect before the first calendar day of that month. Unpaid service is switched off.

The first payment (setup plus first month) may be refunded if the Client asks in writing within 30 days of go-live. After that window, and for every retainer after the first month, there is no refund. A month paid is a month earned.

We may increase fees if our costs rise (Make.com, PDF generation, AI usage, hosting). Existing clients get 30 days' written notice. You may cancel before the new rate starts. The Founder rate does not increase during its 12-month lock. After 12 months it moves to R2,499 per month with 30 days' written notice.
