What exactly is NexAgent OS?

A connected system of eight modules that runs your daily operations: a WhatsApp AI answers customers, quotes are generated and sent, job cards open on acceptance, stock and staff hours update automatically, invoices go out, overdue accounts get chased, and you receive one briefing every morning at 07:00.

What does it cost?

The full OS is R14,999 once-off setup plus R2,499 per month. Individual modules are R4,999 setup plus R999 per month each. The standalone WhatsApp AI Agent is R2,499 setup plus R499 per month.

Do I need to change my WhatsApp number?

No. Your AI runs on its own dedicated South African number — your personal and existing business numbers stay untouched.

What do you need from me?

Your services, pricing and stock list — that's it. The system is built around how your business already works, and you approve everything before go-live.

Is my data safe and POPIA-compliant?

Yes. Your operational data belongs to you, is never sold or shared, and is processed under POPIA-aligned agreements with our automation and AI providers. Full details in our Privacy Policy.
