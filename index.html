SME operations · five AI agents

NexAgent AI

An automated flow system — not a chatbot. From first WhatsApp to quote, job card, stock, timesheet and invoice, the floor runs in one thread.

5 AI agents plus 6 sub-agents that check and finish the work. The agents run on xAI Grok models.

Agents

Front Desk Agent: reads every WhatsApp a customer sends, works out what they need and passes it to the right agent or answers directly.
Quote Agent: prices the job from your parts list, sends a branded quote PDF and checks every quote before it goes out.
Leads Agent: answers 'do you do...?' questions and saves new customers into your leads list automatically.
Job Card Agent: tells customers where their job stands and creates job cards when you text 'create job'.
Invoice Agent: answers customers' invoice questions, builds invoices from your 'create invoice' or 'job done' message and checks each invoice PDF before sending.

Sub-agents

Quote check: checks name, phone and total before a quote PDF goes out.
Invoice check: checks name, phone and total before an invoice PDF goes out.
Create-quote helper: turns the manager's short WhatsApp command into a finished record.
Create-job helper: turns the manager's short WhatsApp command into a finished record.
Create-invoice helper: turns the manager's short WhatsApp command into a finished record.
Part matcher: matches the part named in 'job done', even misspelt, to the stock list.

Feature, not an agent

Timesheets: clock staff in/out and work out day pay by WhatsApp.
